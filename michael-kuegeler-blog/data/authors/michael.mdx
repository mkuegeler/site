Born in Cologne in 1961.

1984-1990 Studied at the Düsseldorf Art Academy under Ulrich Rückriem and Irmin Kamp.

1990 Master student.

1991-1995 Computer-aided design (CAD) for architectural firms in Düsseldorf.

Since 1995 Consultant, manager, and analyst for IT, software, and consulting companies.

Lives and works in Munich.
