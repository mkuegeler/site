## About

My introduction to computer technology began in 1987. 

Towards the end of my studies at the Düsseldorf Art Academy, I was working on folded objects made of hardboard. 

The process required complex geometric calculations. Through friends who were researching in a computer graphics lab, I came into contact with CAD (Computer-Aided Design). 

My journey into the world of computer technology led me to various roles in different industries, initially as a designer in architectural firms, later in software development, telecommunications, and IT consulting. 

Over time, I have collected diverse ideas and conceptual fragments at the crossroads between Technology, Art, Literature, and History.

I hope to dedicate time in the future to transform these notes into finished outputs.
