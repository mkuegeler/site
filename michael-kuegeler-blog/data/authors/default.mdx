I am an IT professional with an artistic background. I am also an occasional artist and writer, as well as an avid reader. I am interested in art, music, politics, history, literature, and technology. I have worked in various roles within the IT industry for most of my life.

Over time, I have accumulated various ideas and thoughts, primarily concerning the intersection of art and technology.

I have been involved with computer technology since the late 1980s and have witnessed the evolution of the digital age firsthand. I am excited to see where artificial intelligence will take us next.

My first passion was drawing. I would spend hours in the attic of my parents' home, surrounded by cherry trees and forests. It was nature that inspired me.

I developed an ambition to become an artist. After graduating, I enrolled in art school, where I explored academic art for a few years. I started with drawing and progressed to creating sculptures.

In 1987, I had my first experience with a computer.

While working on a project involving intensive geometric calculations, I realized that using a computer-aided design (CAD) program could greatly simplify my work.

I was fascinated by the prospect of creating entire virtual worlds with just a few lines of code.

My journey through the computer business began. I witnessed the rise of the Internet and created my first website in 1997.

Since then, I have been eager to learn and experiment with new programming languages and technologies while keeping up to date with the latest trends.

In addition to my daily work in various roles and sectors, such as software development, telecommunications, and IT consulting, I have gathered ideas that I hope to find the time to write down or transform into visual art.
