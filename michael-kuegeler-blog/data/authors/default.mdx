I am an IT professional with an artistic background, occasional writer and avid reader. My interests include art, music, politics, history, literature and technology. I have worked in the IT industry in different fields and roles for most of my life.

Over time, I have gathered a variety of ideas and thoughts that have occurred to me, mostly related to the crossroad between art and technology. 

Having been involved with computer technology since the late 1980s, I have had the opportunity to witness the evolution of the digital age, which is approaching the next level driven by artificial intelligence. 

For the first twenty-five years, I was an analogue person. 

My first passion was drawing. I spent my time in the attic of my parents' home, surrounded by a beautiful landscape of vineyards and cherry trees — nature was my inspiration. 

I developed an ambition to become an artist. After graduating, I enrolled at art school, where I had the opportunity to explore the world of academic art for a few years. I started with drawing and progressed to creating sculptures. 

In 1987, I happened to come into contact with a computer. While working on a project that required intensive geometric calculations, I discovered that using a CAD program could greatly simplify my work.
