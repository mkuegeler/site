Introduction

Last updated: [August 24 2025]

I value your privacy and protect your personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and what rights you have.

What Data I Collect

Contact Data

When you use my contact form or email me, I collect the data you provide (e.g. name, email address, message).

Technical Data

When you visit this website, certain technical information is automatically collected by my hosting provider, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages visited

Cookies

This website may use cookies to improve functionality, analyze usage, or remember user preferences. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

Analytics & Tracking (if enabled)

If I use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics, Matomo, or similar), these may collect information about your browsing behavior (pages visited, time spent, location data, type of device). Analytics cookies will only be set if you consent via the cookie banner.

Embedded Content & Third-Party Services (if used)

Some pages may contain embedded content (e.g. YouTube videos, Google Maps, or social media plugins). These services may set their own cookies and collect user data according to their own privacy policies.

Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

  • Contact requests: To respond to your inquiries (Art. 6(1)(b) or 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Server logging: To ensure website functionality and detect misuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Cookies/Analytics: Based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Embedded services: Based on your consent when you interact with the content (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Data Retention

  • Contact messages: Retained only as long as needed to respond or fulfill legal obligations.
  • Server logs: Typically deleted after 7–30 days, unless needed for security/legal reasons.
  • Cookies: Stored according to their purpose and your browser settings.
  • Analytics Retained in aggregated/anonymous form only, for statistical purposes.

Data Sharing

I only share your data with:

  • Service providers who help me run this website (e.g. hosting providers, analytics providers), with GDPR-compliant agreements in place.
  • Legal authorities, if required by law.

I do not sell or trade your data.

Cookies

You will be informed via a cookie banner and can manage or withdraw your consent at any time. You may also disable cookies in your browser settings. Please note that some site functions may be limited without cookies.

Your Rights

You have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of access (to know what personal data I hold)
  • Right to rectification (correction of errors)
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to data portability

To exercise these rights, contact me at mkuegeler@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Hosting Provider

This website is hosted by IONOS.

The hosting provider automatically processes server log files to ensure stability and security.

A data processing agreement is in place as required by Art. 28 GDPR.

Analytics (Optional Section)

If this site uses Google Analytics or a similar tool:

  • Analytics cookies will only be set with your consent.
  • Data may be transferred to third countries (e.g. the USA). Appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time through the cookie settings.

Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or website services. Please check back periodically for updates.
